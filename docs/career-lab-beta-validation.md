# Career Lab: beta validation

## Product promise

Pick a target role, assess a capability, work on a mapped gap with guided practice, then reassess or attach inspectable work. The roadmap shows the next priority. Study progress is private to this browser and never counts as proof of proficiency.

## Current scope

- Six reviewed Core Notes map to six of fourteen defined competencies; other competencies remain visible in the assessment and roadmap model without a guided note.
- Practice captures a written response and lets the learner compare it against editorial consolidation criteria. The product does not grade free text or verify that the answer is correct.
- Baseline assessments are deterministic probes. Evidence is supplied by the learner; results are not external certification.
- Career data remains local unless the learner exports it. There is no cloud sync.

## Observe five developers before calling the beta ready

Give each person a target role and ask them to use Career Lab without instruction. Observe whether they can: explain what the product does, finish a baseline, find the relevant Core Note, record a meaningful practice response, identify its consolidation criteria, reattempt the assessment or register work, and understand why readiness did or did not change. Let them narrate confusion without correcting it. Repeat on mobile and desktop and check export/import across a fresh browser profile.

Record task completion, where each person hesitated, mistaken expectations about automatic grading or career guarantees, and any lost data. Fix blockers and re-run the affected scenario. Launch criteria: all critical steps complete independently, no data loss in export/import, and participants can explain the distinction between study, assessment and inspectable evidence in their own words. Do not claim this validation is complete until real sessions have taken place.
