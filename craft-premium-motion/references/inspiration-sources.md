## Research method

For a relevant example, record:

- trigger and input mode;
- animated properties and transform layers;
- start/end states;
- approximate duration, easing/spring, delay, and stagger;
- relationship to layout, scroll, and content hierarchy;
- reduced-motion/touch behavior;
- likely rendering cost;
- what must change to fit the current brand.

Do not combine multiple gallery effects merely to increase spectacle. Select one interaction idea that solves the current design problem, then rebuild it within the project's system.
